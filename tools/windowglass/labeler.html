Keys (as in HF Studio)

Left / right buttonAdd glass / remove glass, with every tool
BBrush, a square of 1, 2 or 4 px from the cursor. Shift at the start locks a straight line
RBox: drag to cover, applied when you let go
LLasso: drag a loop, filled when you let go. Alt at release keeps glass only inside it
PPolygon: click corners; Enter, double-click or the first dot closes it
WMagic fill: the same colour around the click (tolerance 60), stops at see-through pixels. Hold and drag to keep filling the same material
TFill the whole 16x16 tile under the cursor
SStamp: drag a box to copy (the "Copy as" button switches to a free shape; Shift while dragging uses the other), then every left click pastes the copy there with the cursor at its centre; right click or Esc drops it. Shift+drag while holding a copy picks a new one. The copy stays when you change window
Ctrl+C / Ctrl+VCopy / paste the 16x16 tile under the cursor
Ctrl+Shift+C / VCopy / paste the whole window's glass (into another window of the same size)
[ ]Brush size
QPaint glass or sheer curtain (light through in part, shown amber). Every tool paints what is chosen
Z, Ctrl+ZUndo · Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z redo
